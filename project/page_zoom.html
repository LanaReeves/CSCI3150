Stations

Station 1 - Downtown

The new station is located at 1015 Oak Street, adjacent to the new City Hall. The new station replaces the Central Fire Station and will be staffed with an engine crew of three and an ambulance crew of two. The new station is the administrative home of fire suppression. Now that the new station is operational, some apparatus will remain in the ambulance barn, and that building will become the home of the seasonal wildland firefighting crews.

Station 2 - Mountain

The Mountain Station is located at 2600 Pine Grove Road for SSFR response to the Mountain, South, and East portions of the district. An engine crew of three and an ambulance crew of two are staffed here daily. The station also has a brush truck, aerial apparatus, and an additional ambulance that are not staffed but available for use as necessary. Fire Prevention is housed within the building.

Ambulance, Search & Rescue Barn

The Ambulance and Search & Rescue Barn is located on 911 Yampa Street. The location houses a two person crew that primarily operates an ambulance but has the ability to operate other fire apparatus as needed. This facility also is home to Routt County Search & Rescue's equipment, offices, meeting space and a command center in the Ambulance Barn.
